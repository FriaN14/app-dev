# Dragon Ball

> *A legendary Japanese franchise about martial arts, adventure, and the search for the Dragon Balls.*

---

## Overview

**Dragon Ball** was created by **Akira Toriyama** and began as a manga in 1984. It follows **Goku**, a Saiyan raised on Earth, as he trains, fights powerful enemies, and protects the planet. The seven **Dragon Balls**, when gathered, summon a dragon that grants a wish.

## Main Series

| Series          | Focus                                   |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Dragon Ball     | Young Goku's adventures and tournaments |
| Dragon Ball Z   | Saiyans, Frieza, Cell, and Majin Buu    |
| Dragon Ball GT  | Anime-only sequel                       |
| Dragon Ball Super | New gods, the Tournament of Power     |

## Key Characters

- **Goku**: the cheerful hero
- **Vegeta**: the proud Saiyan prince and rival
- **Gohan**: Goku's son
- **Piccolo**: former enemy turned ally

## Why It's Popular

1. Epic fights and transformations (like *Super Saiyan*)
2. Themes of friendship, training, and never giving up
3. Huge influence on anime, manga, and games
